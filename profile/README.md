# TradingKnowledgeHub (TKH)

[Read in Chinese](https://github.com/TradingKnowledgeHub/.github/blob/main/profile/README.zh-CN.md)

**Turn trading education into understanding, personal knowledge and playbooks.**

TKH connects stock and options education videos and articles with your notes, questions and AI-assisted research—so what you learn can become knowledge you can revisit and methods you can refine.

## Learn, question, build

- **Study with a purpose.** Explore learning materials and capture the points and questions that matter to you.
- **Work toward understanding.** Discuss the material alongside your notes, keeping source explanations, AI output and your own judgment distinguishable.
- **Build on what you learn.** Organize useful insights into personal Wiki pages and playbooks, with references you can return to and improve.

You decide which conclusions to keep and which methods to adopt.

## Current stage

**Under active development.** TKH is being refined around the learning-to-knowledge workflow. A public installation or testing release is not yet offered here.

## Part of the TAS direction

- **[TradingAlphaSystem (TAS)](https://github.com/TradingAlphaSystem)** is the planned personal trading evolution workspace connecting trading analysis, learning and methods in practice.
- **[TradingAlphaDNA (TAD)](https://github.com/TradingAlphaDNA)** focuses on learning from stock and options trading records.
- **TKH** focuses on learning from educational materials and developing personal knowledge and playbooks.

Cross-product integration is still being developed and evaluated.
